# Mission

[Home](README.md)

The mission of Motoneuron is to keep the model open and accessible for research into ALS and related neurological diseases, and to support collaborative development of methods for their prevention and treatment. Achieving this goal should not end the availability of published versions.

This is a statement of purpose, not an additional license condition or an expiry trigger. From 0.2.0, noncommercial scientific research is permitted without a fee; commercial use requires a separate agreement with both Licensors or their jointly authorized representative. Here, "open" describes access to source for research, not OSI open-source status. Version 0.1.2 retains AGPL-3.0-only. Reaching the mission's goal does not end the license permissions. This statement does not promise perpetual hosting or free third-party services or future medicines.
